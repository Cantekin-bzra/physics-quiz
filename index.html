Before you start

This quiz is built around misconceptions, not arithmetic. Each wrong option is a specific way of thinking that feels right but breaks down. If you pick one, you will see exactly where the reasoning goes wrong.

  • Six questions, one from each topic of the course
  • Immediate feedback after every answer
  • A short explanatory video for each wrong option
  • A personal review list at the end
